About Circle Arc L1 Network

An open Layer-1 blockchain purpose-built for stablecoin-native financial applications and global liquidity.

Stablecoin-Native Gas

Arc uses USDC directly as its native gas token, eliminating price volatility for transaction fees and ensuring predictable $0.001 micro-fees.

Sub-Second Finality

Engineered for high-frequency institutional finance, Arc provides sub-second deterministic finality and support for thousands of parallel transactions per second.

Permissioned Validators

Secured by world-class institutions including Circle, BlackRock, Visa, DTCC, BNY, and Mastercard for strict regulatory alignment and market trust.

Native CCTP Bridge

Direct integration with Circle's Cross-Chain Transfer Protocol allows seamless 1:1 USDC movement across Ethereum, Solana, Arbitrum, and Avalanche.
