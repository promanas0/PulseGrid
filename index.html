Circle Arc L1 Architecture

About Circle Arc L1 Network

Arc is an open, stablecoin-native Layer-1 blockchain purpose-built by Circle to unite programmable money, economic contracts, and onchain capital markets with sub-second deterministic finality.

Stablecoin-Native Gas

Arc uses USDC directly as its native gas token, eliminating price volatility for transaction fees and ensuring predictable $0.001 micro-fees for all developers and institutional applications.

ERC-8004 & ERC-8183 Agent Protocols

Native support for AI agent identity registration (ERC-8004) and trustless USDC escrow job settlement (ERC-8183) for the agentic economy.

Permissioned Consortium Validators

Secured by world-class financial institutions including Circle, BlackRock, Visa, DTCC, BNY, and Mastercard for strict regulatory alignment and global trust.

Native CCTP Interoperability

Direct protocol-level integration with Circle's Cross-Chain Transfer Protocol allows seamless 1:1 USDC movement across Ethereum, Solana, Arbitrum, and Avalanche.
