Agent Ai is the infrastructure that revenue teams use to deploy agents, unify data, and automate workflows across their entire go-to-market stack. Think of it as the operating system for your revenue motion.

Most tools solve one piece of the puzzle — a CRM, an enrichment vendor, a routing tool. Agent Ai unifies all of these into a single platform with a shared data layer and agent infrastructure. Your team works from one system of truth instead of patching together five.

Agent Ai is built for revenue operations, sales, and marketing teams at growing B2B companies. Our customers range from 20-person startups to 1000+ person public companies.

Most teams are up and running in 1–3 weeks. Simple setups can launch in days; complex migrations from multiple legacy tools typically take 4–6 weeks with our solutions team supporting you the entire way.

Agent Ai is priced based on the number of seats and the volume of records in your data layer. We offer transparent pricing on our website, with custom Enterprise plans for teams with specific compliance or scale requirements.

Yes. Agent Ai is SOC 2 Type II certified, audited annually by an independent third party. We're also GDPR and CCPA compliant, with HIPAA-ready options available for Enterprise customers.

All data is encrypted in transit using TLS 1.3 and at rest using AES-256. Encryption keys are managed via AWS KMS with automatic rotation. You can read more in our Security page.

Yes — SSO/SAML is included on every plan. We support Okta, Google Workspace, Microsoft Entra ID, and any SAML 2.0 provider out of the box.

Agent Ai offers bi-directional sync with all major CRMs (Salesforce, HubSpot), communication tools (Slack, Gmail, Outlook), enrichment vendors (Clearbit, Apollo, ZoomInfo), and data warehouses (Snowflake, BigQuery). See our full integrations directory for the complete list.
